## Engineered to rank
 Google weighs Core Web Vitals and crawlable, semantic HTML in its rankings. Astro outputs both by default — pre-rendered static pages, minimal client JS, clean markup. The SEO groundwork is in the architecture, not bolted on afterward.
## A minimal attack surface by design
 Most website hacks happen through databases and plugins. Your site doesn't use either, so there's simply far less that can be broken into. Fewer weak points, fewer things to worry about.
## Loads in under a second
 Over half of mobile visitors abandon a site that takes more than three seconds to load. Our sites, served as static pages from Cloudflare's edge, are built to turn casual traffic into paying customers.
## Actively maintained, not left to rot
 Astro, dependencies, and integrations are kept current — security patches and framework updates applied deliberately, tested against a preview deploy before they ever hit production. Your site stays modern instead of aging into a liability.
## Edge-delivered, globally
 Your site is distributed across Cloudflare's network and served from the node closest to each visitor. Local customers get instant loads; you're also fast for anyone, anywhere, with no origin server to fall over under traffic.
## Direct line to the developer
 You work with the person writing the code — not an account manager, not an offshore team. Architecture decisions, changes, and fixes come from one Bundaberg-based developer who knows your entire stack.

 FAQs
## Frequently Asked Questions
 Here are the questions I hear most often about Rebirth Online. If your question isn’t answered below, feel free to reach out—I’m happy to chat.
## Why choose Rebirth Online for my project?
 I focus on fast, modern static websites built with Astro and Tailwind CSS—delivering standout design, blazing speed, and 90+ Lighthouse scores out of the box. Every site is mobile-first, accessible, SEO-ready, and tailored to your vision without unnecessary complexity or bloat.
## What kinds of projects do you take on?
 My core expertise is in high-quality static sites: portfolios, business landing pages, marketing sites, documentation hubs, and personal brands. I’m also open to light dynamic features (e.g., forms, search, or simple APIs) when they add real value, all while keeping performance and maintainability first.
## How long does it take to build a website?
 A focused prototype can be ready in just a few days. Full custom sites typically take 2–4 weeks from kickoff to launch, depending on scope and feedback rounds. I work iteratively—quick wins early, refinements along the way—so you see progress fast.
## What do you need from me to get started?
 Clear goals, any existing branding/assets (logo, colors, photos), content (or a plan for it), and reference sites you love. If you’re not sure about any of that, I can guide you through discovery and help shape the direction.
## How much does a website cost?
 Pricing is custom and transparent based on your needs. I offer a limited number of pro-bono or heavily discounted sites each year to build momentum and portfolio pieces. Paid projects start in the mid-four-figure range for bespoke, high-performance sites. I’ll provide a detailed quote after our initial discussion.
## Do you offer maintenance or updates after launch?
 Yes. I can hand off the full source code for you or your team to manage, or I can provide ongoing support (updates, new sections, performance tweaks) on a monthly retainer or per-request basis—whatever fits your workflow best.
